Briefly describe the stages of working with a client from initial inquiry to transaction completion for the "Work Process" block:
Client Work Process Stages:
1. Initial Consultation: Getting to know the client, determining their goals, budget, preferences regarding real estate, and future plans.
2. Country and Region Selection: Conducting a detailed analysis of the client's needs and helping select a country and region that best suit their goals — for living, relocation, investing, vacation, generating rental income, or capital preservation.
3. Individual Property Selection: Selecting apartments, houses, villas, land plots, or commercial real estate from vetted developers and partners.
4. Presentation and Property Choice: Providing detailed information on each option, helping compare properties, evaluate their advantages, and select the best solution.
5. Viewing Organization: Organizing online or offline viewings of selected properties and coordinating communication with the seller or developer.
6. Legal Due Diligence: Organizing the verification of documents, property legal status, and transaction conditions together with local lawyers and partners.
7. Negotiations and Reservation: Assisting in negotiations regarding price and purchase terms, helping reserve the selected property.
8. Transaction Support: Coordinating document processing, contract signing, payments, and ownership registration.
9. Property Handover: Accompanying the client until documents and keys are received.
10.After-Sales Support: Helping with furnishing, renovation, rental listing, property management, as well as consulting on relocation, adaptation, and obtaining temporary or permanent residence permits (if applicable programs exist in the country) as needed.
My goal is to make the process of buying real estate abroad as simple, safe, and comfortable as possible, providing professional guidance at every stage.
