About Me

Dedicated to your success, wherever you're investing.

I built G Vision on a simple idea: real estate should feel personal, even when it crosses borders. Over two decades, I've helped individuals and families buy, sell, and invest in property across a growing list of international markets — combining local market knowledge with a genuinely hands-on approach.

Every client relationship starts with listening. From there, I bring in the right network, the right data, and the right amount of patience to get the outcome you're after.

  • Licensed across multiple jurisdictions
  • Personal, hands-on approach to every deal
  • Established international partner network
